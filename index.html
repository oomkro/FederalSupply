Section 01

Project Vision
& Approach

The Mission

Move Federal Supply onto a modern Shopify B2B foundation. Built on Makro's components, and connected to the NetSuite migration already underway. Faster, cleaner, and easier for your team to run.

What this means for you
.01

A platform built for B2B ordering

Catalog, accounts, and ordering on a foundation purpose-built for repeat-buyer businesses, not bolted on top of one.

.02

A cleaner buyer experience

Faster and more usable than the current site for the customers you have today.

.03

Connected operations

Shopify and NetSuite stay in sync. Less copy-paste between systems, fewer places for data to drift.

Built on Makro Components.

Our accelerator approach uses proven, pre-built Shopify B2B foundations, extended where it matters for Federal Supply rather than rebuilt from scratch. Three principles guide how we work together.

Principle 01

Component-Based

The B2B foundation, account workflows, and storefront patterns come from our Shopify accelerator playbook. They've been proven across previous launches. We extend them, we don't reinvent them.

Principle 02

Collaborative

The work is in the choices we make together. What stays standard, what gets tailored for Federal Supply, and what's deferred to phase two. We work as one team.

Principle 03

Lift & Modernize

The first goal is to get Federal Supply onto better infrastructure, quickly. We optimize where it makes a difference. We don't redesign what already works.

Section 02

Phases
& Process

Project Phase
Breakdown.

Four phases, sequenced over approximately 16 weeks. Each phase has clear deliverables and sign-off gates before we move to the next. We'll walk through specifics in the scope-review session.

Phase 01

Onboarding & Discovery

We meet, align, and gather what we need to start. Questionnaires and topic workshops set the foundation before design begins.

We are here
Phase 02

Strategy & Design

Information architecture, user experience, and visual design come together. By the end, we have a clear blueprint and approved direction.

Phase 03

Build & Integration

Pages, features, and the integrations that connect them are built in prioritized batches. The team works in two-week sprints with regular check-ins.

Phase 04

Testing & Launch

End-to-end QA, structured client UAT, then cutover and a focused hypercare window to support the team through the first weeks of go-live.
